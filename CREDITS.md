# Credits

NoSwap is built on other people's work. Thank you all.

## Sprites

Every character is drawn by the artists below; NoSwap only crops, mirrors and places frames from their sheets (and, where a
sheet's colours don't fit the game's palette, merges its rarest shades, as each character's `SOURCE.txt` says). The
sheets are not in this repository: [testmods/SHEETS.md](testmods/SHEETS.md) links each one, and each character's
`testmods/<folder>/SOURCE.txt` has the full credit and the sheet's own terms.

## Code, tools and research

- **Sonic Mania decompilation** and **RSDKv5(U) decompilation**: Rubberduckycooly and chuliRMG; original RSDK by
  Evening Star (https://github.com/RSDKModding). NoSwapMania is a mod for it and builds against RSDKv5-GameAPI;
  `tools/rsdk5_extract.py` ports its data pack reader. See native/mania/LICENSE-DECOMP.md.
- **The RSDKv3 / RSDKv4 decompilations** (Rubberduckycooly, chuliRMG and contributors): how the Sonic 1, 2 and CD
  scripts and engine behave.
- **HedgeModManager** and **HiteModLoader** (hedge-dev): loading NoSwap into Sonic Origins, and the decompiled scripts the
  build starts from. HiteModLoader's headers are in native/third_party (MIT).
- **MinHook** by Tsuda Kageyu: function hooking (native/third_party/minhook, BSD 2-Clause).
- **thesupersonic16's DllMods** (Origins Ultrafix's `Ultrafix3kFixes`, `OriginsPlayerPatcher`): the reference for many
  RSDK and Sonic 3 & Knuckles types and signatures (native/third_party/reference, MIT).
- **HedgeLib** by Radfordhound and **HedgeArcPack**: the PACx archive layout.
- **DeaTh-G's surfboard templates**: the SurfRide (SWIF) structures.
- **RSDKv5Extract**: Sonic Mania data pack file names.
- **vgmstream**: decoding Origins' sound banks (tools/origins_sfx.py).
- The Sonic Origins and Sonic Mania modding communities, whose tools and research this builds on.

NoSwap itself: directed, designed and tested by Superevil, with the code and reverse engineering written with heavy AI
assistance (Claude, by Anthropic). See "About AI use" in the README.
