# Example: Bean the Dynamite

NoSwap's own Bean, as a complete `character.json`: frames, animations (Origins and Sonic 2's extra ones), his moves (Leap
double jump, bomb throw), palette, HUD, signpost, ending poses, and Mania enabled.

**His sprite sheet isn't included.** It's "Bean the Dynamite (Sonic 1 Style)" by **deltaConduit**, on The Spriters
Resource: https://www.spriters-resource.com/custom_edited/sonicthehedgehogcustoms/asset/263920/

1. Copy this `bean` folder into your characters folder (`characters/` in the kit's data folder: the kit's README,
   "Where things are").
2. Download the sheet from that page and save it in the folder as **`Bean.png`**. It's 693x529, with a lavender
   background; the frames in `character.json` are measured on it (Check says so if your copy differs).
3. Open the folder in the editor, look around, and Build.

The sheet's terms, printed on it: "Original sprites by SEGA, Sonic Team & deltaConduit. Free to use, but give credit. Do
not edit. Don't use these sprites for .exe stuff." So: frames are crops only, nothing recoloured or redrawn; credit
deltaConduit whenever you share something made with it. The key here is `example.bean`: give your own characters your
own key (`<you>.<character>`).
