Credit (README): Original sprites by SEGA, Sonic Team & Akimaca, with the link above. Public. Bomb (c) SEGA.

What we do: frames are cut out of their cells as drawn (he already faces right; nothing is mirrored); the UI art
(life counter, signpost, continue icons) as drawn. Only the "Normal" palette; not the palette variants or the
"Scrapped/Test" box. The Extras (Detonating, Explosion) make his Self-Destruct. His jump / Spin Dash / special stage ball is
Sonic Mania's plain spin ball (SEGA / Sonic Team) in his colours, resized nearest-neighbour to 24 px (tools/generic_ball.py, not the artist's art): his Labyrinth spin is him turning round, not a ball. No scaling except the EXPLOSION
frames, a pixel-exact 2x (nearest-neighbour) so the blast drawn matches its reach; no crops beyond a cell; every
colour exact.
