Sheet legend: dark blue = "2013 exclusive", blue = "S1F exclusive", purple = "New sprites".

Usage terms: none stated on the sheet beyond the credits; credit everyone above.
What we do: frames are only cut (whole boxes; every box background colour, and the lime ground/wall marker
bars, made transparent). Nothing is redrawn, resized, rotated or recoloured. Every colour keeps its exact value
in a palette slot of its own (74-94); only black (#000000, and #080000) shares Sonic's slot 1. The HUD icon,
"TRIP" name tag, continue minis and signpost board are the sheet's own; the signpost board sits under the
game's own 2-row pole cap from Items2.gif.
