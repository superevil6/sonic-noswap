Layout: a page (#3f48cc) of Genesis hardware sprite boxes (dark teal #002424; navy #00006c: the same frame on the other
priority layer), in sections: TITLE (the big title-screen Headdy: not used), then IN-GAME: BODY 1-10 (his headless body
poses) and HEAD 1-20 (head sets), SCENE 3-2, SPOTLIGHT, USA only, END (JPN / USA). The body's green neck bolt (#00fc00) is
where the game puts the head.

What we do (make_configs.py): Genesis art at 1x. Headdy is a composite, as in his game: each frame is a body with a head
put on at its neck bolt (the head box's bottom, 12 px in from its right edge, on the bolt), both copied exactly into
build/source.png. The thrown head is drawn at runtime (tools/head_throw.py), his body headless meanwhile. Crops, mirroring
and placing existing pieces only; nothing redrawn, recoloured or resized. The HUD tag "HEADDY" is our lettering in the HUD
font the other extras use; the life icon / 1-UP are a crop of his standing head, the signpost that head on the game's
own board. All 11 colours exact (black in Sonic's slot 1, the rest in 74-83). The frames used are listed in make_configs.py.
