Not used: the "PHOTO" at the top right (SEGA's official art: never used), the little signature portrait at the bottom
left, the Chaos Emerald icons and the sheet's lettering.

What we do: the sprites (the rise from a puddle, HIT, ATACK, RUN, FALL, JUMP) are cut from the sheet and chosen,
repeated or mirrored as stand-ins (make_configs.py lists which frame plays what), and enlarged 1.1x with
nearest-neighbour (he read as short; the user approved this for him only, 2026-09-27). The HUD icons are 16-px crops of his standing
head; the signpost shows that pose's head and shoulders on the game's own board (Items2). The "CHAOS" HUD name tag
isn't on the sheet: our own lettering in the HUD font the other extras use. His jump, Spin Dash and special-stage ball
is Sonic Mania's plain spin ball (SEGA / Sonic Team) in his blues (tools/generic_ball.py, not the artist's art; it replaced his puddle). His Origins select card is the sheet's larger
"PIXEL ART" Chaos (51x96, the same artist's), cropped as-is and enlarged 4x with nearest-neighbour (pixel-exact).
Palette: the built frames use 15 colours, each kept exactly in the extras' own slots 74-88. Nothing is merged,
redrawn, smoothed or recoloured.
