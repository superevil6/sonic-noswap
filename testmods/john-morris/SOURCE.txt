Credit in release: John Morris (Castlevania: Bloodlines): original rip by Badbatman3, re-ripped by UltraHype97; items and
sub-weapons extracted by Yawackhary; The Spriters Resource; sprites (c) Konami.
A separate download (extras.py "crossover"): shipped only as his own mod, never in the all-in-one bundle.

What we do (make_configs.py): Genesis art at 1x (he stands 42-46 px). Frames are cut whole, as drawn (build/source.png is a
working copy with each used frame's pixels copied exactly into a cell of its own). The whip is baked, not drawn at runtime:
the sheet's "Assembled whip anims." rows show the LV1 leather whip in place round grey silhouettes of his attack frames; for
each of them his real attack frame is put where its silhouette is (the offset where their shapes overlap best) and the
whip's own pixels, as that row places them, go on top (existing pixels pasted together; nothing redrawn). Ground (ATK2-4),
crouch (CATK2-4), aim up (AIMUP1-2, the whip diagonally up) and aim down (AIMDN1-2, the whip hanging straight down, as the
sheet has it). The sub-weapons are Items.png's own drawings, cut as drawn: the thrown axe (its upright and level drawings,
each also turned half round: a full turn in four frames), the cross (Bloodlines' boomerang-cross, its four red spinning
drawings), the tossed holy water vial and the blue flames (the wide and the tall flame). Items.png's colours are the same
Genesis colours as the John sheet's, converted a shade differently (e.g. #210021 / #200020): they draw with his nearest own
colour (within 5 of 255 per channel). Not used: the stairs, the grapple / swing (not wanted), the Death palettes (a palette
dissolve), whips LV2-4 (no upgrades), the tweaked sprites, the other items. The HUD icon is his idle frame's head cropped to
16x16; the signpost is that head on the game's own board, at 1x. The "JOHN" HUD tag isn't on the sheet: our lettering in
the HUD font the other extras use. His colours are the sheet's 15, all exact in his own slots (none merged). Nothing is
redrawn, recoloured or resized.
