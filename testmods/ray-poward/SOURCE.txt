A CROSSOVER character (extras.py "crossover": a separate download only, never in the all-in-one bundle). Not Ray the
Flying Squirrel (testmods/ray, key noswap.ray): this one is testmods/ray-poward, key noswap.ray-poward, "RAY POWARD".

What we do (make_configs.py): Genesis art at 1x (he stands 40 px). Frames are cut whole, as drawn (build/source.png is a
working copy with each used frame's pixels copied exactly into a cell of its own). His jump and roll are the sheet's own
somersault (four quarter turns of one drawing, as the sheet has them); no ball. The projectile is the sheet's weapon A
bullet (6x6), cut exactly; its muzzle flashes, the laser, B, C and D aren't used. The signpost is the Misc portrait
(inside its frame) on the game's own board, at 1x; the life icon / 1-UP are crops of the Misc standing frame's head.
The "POWARD" HUD tag isn't on the sheet: our lettering in the HUD font the other extras use. Colours: his 16 exact in
slots 74-89 (black is Sonic's slot 1), the bullet's and the portrait's shades exact in 90-95; one 1-px shade (#e0e0e0,
the bullet's glint) merged into #f8f8f8. Nothing redrawn, recoloured or resized.
