A separate download (extras.py "crossover": never in the all-in-one bundle).

What we do (make_configs.py): Genesis art at 1x. Frames are cut whole, as drawn (build/source.png is a working copy with
each used frame's pixels copied exactly into a cell of its own). His arms are drawn at runtime as the game draws them:
two 2 px black lines from his body to his hands (a runtime draw effect, tools/star_grab.py); his hands are the sheet's
loose hands, turned by quarter turns and mirrored for 8 directions. The Meteor Strike row is turned by quarter turns for
its directions. The wind-up is 8 of the 48 swing-on-handle frames, placed by the handle's ball. The HUD icon / 1-UP are
a crop of the standing frame's face; the signpost is his star head on the game's own board at 1x. The "RISTAR" HUD tag
isn't on the sheet: our lettering in the HUD font the other extras use. All 15 colours exact (black in Sonic's slot 1,
the rest in 74-87); nothing redrawn, recoloured or resized.
