Usage terms: free to use with credit; no edits, no recolours, not for ".EXE" (creepypasta) projects.
What we do: frames are only cut. Nothing is redrawn, resized or recoloured. Every colour keeps its exact value
in a palette slot of its own (74-94), so no game can swap it for its own nearest shade; only black (#000000)
shares Sonic's slot 1. Near-identical duplicate shades on the sheet merge into their twin's slot:
#080000 -> #000000, #919191 -> #909090, #464646 -> #484848, #910000 -> #900000,
#b5b5b5 -> #b4b4b4, #fcb591 / #fcb691 -> #fcb490, #b56c46 / #b66c46 -> #b46c48. The HUD name tag
"ROBOTNIK" isn't on the sheet; it is our own lettering in the game's HUD style, not an edit of his sprites.
Credit in release: Dr. Cheesecrumbz (sheet); original sprites by SEGA / Sonic Team and the other games' makers.
#202020 (9 pixels) has its own slot 94 rather than merging into black.
