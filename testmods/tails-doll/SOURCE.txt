What we do: frames are cut as drawn (no scaling: he stands 35 px tall with his antenna); the HANG ROLL pair and one spin

body frames where the sheet's cells put them (make_configs.py). The HUD tag (icon and "TAILS DOLL"), 1-UP picture,
signpost board, continue minis and endings (small and big) are the sheet's own. Palette: 22 colours exact in slots
74-95, black in Sonic's slot 1; merged (the colour exception): the near-black #080000 into black, #902400 (3 px) into
#880000, and the signpost's and minis' shades 1-3 levels off into their twins.
