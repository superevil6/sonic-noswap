"Gamma (E-102 Gamma)" by Nebula, contributor Cylent-Nite (submitted 2017-09-07). No usage terms on the page.

Text on the sheet (verbatim):
  CREDIT TO CYLENT NITE FOR ORIGINAL GAMMA SPRITES
  CREDIT TO NEBULA FOR SPRITES
  CREDIT TO QUICKSILVER TAI ALLISTER FOR POSES
  CREDIT TO HC MISSILE FOR ORIGINAL BOOSTER AND CLAW
  (and the logo "A Cylent Nite Creation")
Usage terms: none beyond that; credit all four, never pass the sprites off as ours.

What we do: only the machine-gun block (the bottom one, y 1520+) is used, its right-facing half. Frames are cut
whole; build/source.png is a working copy with each used frame's pixels copied exactly into a cell of its own
(the hover frames' separate booster-flame blobs kept where the sheet draws them). The large ending pose is an exact
2x nearest-neighbour enlargement. Nothing is redrawn, recoloured, filtered or shrunk. The "GAMMA" HUD tag isn't on
the sheet; it is our lettering in the game's HUD style (the font the other extras' tags use).
Colours: the used frames have 28 colours and the extras have 22 own palette slots (74-95). #0c0c0c and #464646 go
to Sonic's 1 (#080000) and 9 (#404040); 22 colours are exact in own slots; #b0b0b0 -> #aeaeae, #ececec -> #f4f4f4,
#3b5ad8 -> #2c7cec and #03593c -> #009500 are merged (make_configs.py prints the pixel counts).
Credit in release: Cylent Nite (original Gamma sprites), Nebula (sprites), Quicksilver Tai Allister (poses),
HC Missile (original booster and claw).
E-102 Gamma is SEGA / Sonic Team's.
