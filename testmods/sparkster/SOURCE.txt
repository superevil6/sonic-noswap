A separate download (extras.py "crossover"): shipped only as his own mod, never in the all-in-one bundle.

What we do (make_configs.py): Genesis art at 1x (he stands about 40-44 px). Frames are cut whole, as drawn (build/source.png
is a working copy with each used frame's pixels copied exactly into a cell of its own: the main drawing and the small loose
bits beside it, e.g. the sword's swooshes). The Rocket Dash's five angles are the sheet's own (its up frame is the sheet's
reused frame); his charge flash alternates the sheet's Crouch and Backfire frames. The energy wave is the sheet's own
Projectile frames (four 32x24 cells, cut as drawn). Not used: the pole hangs (no hang move, the user), swimming, the
cutscene vehicles, the effects. The HUD icon is his face cropped to 16x16 from the Pleased cutscene frame; the signpost is
his head from that frame on the game's own board, at 1x. The "SPARKSTER" HUD tag isn't on the sheet: our lettering in the
HUD font the other extras use. His colours are the sheet's 15 plus black, all exact in his own slots (none merged).
Nothing is redrawn, recoloured or resized.
