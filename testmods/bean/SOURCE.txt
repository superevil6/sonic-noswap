Sonic's height), no recolouring, no merged colours (15 colours, each exact: black in Sonic's slot 1, 14 in slots
74-87), no pieces put together into new frames, nothing .exe-themed. One crop beyond a cell: the Sonic 1 ending's medium
pose (ENDING 7, 77x65) loses its 6 leftmost columns (the tips of his tail feathers) to fit the pose box every package
shares. The HUD icon and "BEAN" tag (1-UP), 1-UP picture, signpost board (GOAL SIGN), continue icons (CONTINUE ICON) and
ending poses (ENDING, the big one as drawn) are the sheet's own. His bomb (the shot) is the sheet's loose bomb beside
IDLE, cut as-is and shown mirrored every few frames.
