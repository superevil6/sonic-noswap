Usage terms: give credit to Gussprint; never claim the sprites as ours.
Credit in release: E-123 Omega sprites custom sprited by Gussprint (Sonic Battle style); the flame-shot fireball is the
sheet's Shot Attack SFX, which the sheet credits to SNK/Playmore; the hover's jet flames are its Midair Action SFX.
E-123 Omega (c) SEGA / Sonic Team.

What we do: only the right-facing rows are used; frames are cut whole (build/source.png is a working copy with each used
frame's pixels copied exactly into a cell of its own). The hover's jet flames are the sheet's own flame drawings placed
under his feet in his fall pose. The fireball projectile is two frames of the Shot Attack SFX, each cropped to the ball
and a little of its flame (the charged Flame Blast); the small Flame Shot is two whole frames of the Aim Attack SFX
(its flame puffs), cut exactly, not scaled. The charge's red-orange flash is a runtime palette effect in his own
colours (the files keep his own). The Origins card is his idle frame with one column off each side (a crop), 8x. Nothing is
redrawn, recoloured or resized. Colours: his frames' 15 and 7 of the fireball's are exact in the 22 own slots; the
fireball's rarer shades and the jet flames' 4 colours (which the Aim Attack puffs share) go to their nearest slot (make_configs.py prints the counts).
The "OMEGA" HUD tag isn't on the sheet: our lettering in the HUD font the other extras use.
