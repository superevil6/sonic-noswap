Joe Musashi (Shinobi III / The Super Shinobi II, Sega Mega Drive / Genesis)

Sheet: Shinobi3_JoeMusashi.png, ripped by Eddo (contributor Kiselyok), The Spriters Resource, asset 20808
(https://www.spriters-resource.com/sega_genesis/shinobi3/asset/20808/).
Its own words (the sheet's footer): "Joe Musashi from the Super Shinobi II (AKA Shinobi III) ripped by Eddo. All
sprites are from the final version of the game, beta sprites will be on a different sheet. Shinobi and all sprites
here are owned by Sega, so no credit needed, but do not steal or claim as your own."

Credited anyway (README, the Origins card: "Sprites: Eddo (tSR)").

Used as drawn (faithful art rule): crops of his frames, no scaling (he's about 60 px tall against Sonic's 40; the user
allows integer scaling only, so he stays his own size, as the taller extras do). His shuriken is the thrown blade from
his own throw frame (188,164, 9x1). His Ninjutsu icons are the games' own monitor pictures (no art drawn), his spin
ball Sonic Mania's plain ball (SEGA) in his colours. Mania: 3 rare colours (#90906c, #904848, #240000) merge into the
nearest global colour (the palette limit; to confirm with the user).
