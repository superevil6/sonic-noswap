# NoSwap: Extra Characters

NoSwap adds new playable characters to all four games in Sonic Origins: **Sonic the Hedgehog**, **Sonic CD**, **Sonic the Hedgehog 2** and **Sonic 3 & Knuckles**. It doesn't replace anyone: Sonic, Tails, Knuckles and Amy are all still there, and the new characters are extra picks on top of them.

Each one has their own sprites, HUD icon, signpost, results name and save files, and most have their own moves.

This is a free fan mod, made for fun. It isn't supported or updated, so enjoy it as it is.

## Requirements

- Sonic Origins on PC (Steam).
- [HedgeModManager](https://github.com/hedge-dev/HedgeModManager) 8 or newer. It sets up the mod loader and the decompiled game scripts this mod builds on.
- Tested with Sonic Origins Plus.
- **Not compatible with Sonic Origins Ultrafix (yet).** Both mods replace many of the same game scripts, so running them together crashes. Disable Ultrafix while playing NoSwap. Other mods that replace the player, enemy or monitor scripts may clash the same way.

## Installing

1. Open HedgeModManager and pick Sonic Origins.
2. Install the mod. Either use HedgeModManager's install option on the downloaded archive, or copy the `NoSwap` folder into the game's `mods` folder.
3. Tick **NoSwap** in the mod list, then click **Save & Play**.

## Playing as an extra character

- **Sonic 1, Sonic 2 and Sonic CD:** the extras are in Origins' own character select, after Sonic, Tails, Knuckles and Amy. Choose Classic or Anniversary mode, start the game, and the character select opens as usual. Move right past the last card to scroll through all the extras (and left past the first to go back). Each extra has their own save, their own progress and their own "continue".
- **Sonic 3 & Knuckles:** on the game's own save screen, press **up/down** on a save slot to cycle its character. The extras come after Amy & Tails. Each slot remembers its extra, and so does "No Save".

The extras' saves are kept in their own file, next to Origins' saves (`SEGA\SonicOrigins\NoSwap\extras.sav` in your AppData folder), so Origins' own save file is never changed by them.

## Moves

All extras keep Sonic's basic moves: running, rolling, the spin jump and the Spin Dash. On top of that, most have their own.

Y moves work in all four games. In Sonic CD, looking up and pressing jump also does the ones that work on the ground.

### Metal Sonic

- **Jet Dash:** press jump in mid-air to blast forward at full speed. The dash hits enemies.
- **Hover:** keep holding jump after the dash to hover for a moment.
- **Robot:** he never needs to breathe underwater, in all four games (no countdown, no drowning music).
- **Super form:** with all the Chaos Emeralds and 50 rings, jump and press Y (Sonic 1, Sonic 2 and Sonic 3 & Knuckles).
- **Physics:** higher top speed than Sonic, but slower to get going.

### Fang the Sniper

- **Tail pogo:** press jump in mid-air to bounce on his tail. Hold jump to keep bouncing on every landing. The pogo hits enemies and breaks monitors.
- **Cork gun:** press Y to fire a cork from his popgun, aimed with the d-pad: on the ground back, up, forward or the diagonals between; in the air all eight ways. With no direction held it fires the way he faces. The cork flies straight until it hits something, reaches a wall, floor or ceiling, or runs out after a short distance; it breaks enemies and monitors and hits bosses. One at a time, every two seconds.

### Big the Cat

- **Umbrella:** press jump in mid-air to open his umbrella, and hold jump to drift down slowly.
- **Swim:** underwater, press jump in mid-air to paddle upward. Keep pressing to keep swimming, with a short pause between strokes. (Underwater he doesn't open his umbrella.)
- **Fishing Cast:** press Y to cast his lure. Hold a direction as you press it to aim: back, back-up, up, forward-up or forward, on the ground or in the air; nothing held casts forward. It's a short cast that hits the first enemy, monitor or boss it reaches. One at a time, every two seconds. He plants his feet to cast on the ground; in the air he keeps his speed.
- **Physics:** heavy. He's slow to get moving, but he jumps higher than Sonic.

### Shadow the Hedgehog

- **Chaos Control:** press jump in mid-air. He freezes for a moment, warps forward, then hops. It hits enemies.
- **Chaos Spear:** press Y to fire a bolt of energy straight ahead, the way he faces, on the ground or in the air. It flies until it hits something, meets a wall or fades out after a moment, and up to two can be out at once. He keeps running while he fires.
- **Physics:** a little faster than Sonic.

### Blaze the Cat

- **Burst Dash:** press jump in mid-air to dash wrapped in flame. Hold up or down as you press it to aim the dash 45 degrees up or down. It hits enemies.
- **Fireproof:** fire never hurts her, as if she always had a Fire Shield (only its protection: no shield, no fire dash). Lava, fireballs, flamethrowers and fire-breathing bosses leave her alone: Marble Zone's lava and fireballs, Hill Top's lava, and the other fire hazards in Sonic 1 and 2; the fireballs in Collision Chaos and Metallic Madness in Sonic CD; and in Sonic 3 & Knuckles everything a Fire Shield protects from, including Lava Reef's lava, which she can stand on. Everything else still hurts her.
- **Physics:** a little faster and quicker off the mark than Sonic.

### Silver the Hedgehog

- **Psychokinesis:** press jump in mid-air, then hold it to float down slowly, for up to 2 seconds per jump.
- **Psychic Wave:** press Y for a short burst of psychic energy. It hits enemies and monitors close by.
- **Physics:** a little slower than Sonic, with a slightly higher jump.

### Mighty the Armadillo

- **Hammer Drop** (as in Sonic Mania Plus): press jump in mid-air to slam straight down. Landing bounces him back up in his shell.

### Ray the Flying Squirrel

- **Glide** (as in Sonic Mania Plus): press jump in mid-air and keep holding it to fly. Hold back to swoop up and forward to dive; each swoop is a little weaker than the last. Let go of jump to drop.

### Rouge the Bat

- **Glide and climb:** Rouge plays like Knuckles. Press jump in mid-air to glide, steer with left/right, and glide into a wall to cling on and climb. Her glide is a bat's: slower than Knuckles', but she sinks much more gently, so it carries her further. She also breaks walls like Knuckles and uses his physics. In Sonic 3 & Knuckles she plays Knuckles' game.
- **Screw Kick:** press Y in mid-air, even while gliding, to dive down and forward in a spinning kick until she lands, then bounce up a little. It hits enemies and breaks monitors, once per jump; a kick out of a glide ends the glide.
- **Treasure Sense:** press Y while standing, walking or running. Rouge stops briefly to listen. Then, for about 2.5 seconds, a blinking ring points at the nearest treasure. It sits at the edge of the screen in the treasure's direction, or on the treasure itself once it's in view. The closer she gets, the faster it blinks, like a metal detector. The treasure is:
  - in Sonic 1, the giant ring at the end of the act, or the nearest unopened monitor if there's no giant ring;
  - in Sonic 2, the nearest unopened monitor;
  - in Sonic CD, the big ring, or the nearest unopened monitor if there's no big ring;
  - in Sonic 3 & Knuckles, the nearest hidden giant ring.

  If there's nothing to find, a soft sound plays and no ring appears. She can use it again a second after it ends.
- **Jewel Thief:** ring monitors give her 20 rings instead of 10. Other monitors are unchanged.

### Charmy Bee

- **Flight:** Charmy plays like Tails. Press jump in mid-air to fly, and keep pressing it to climb, until he tires out. In Sonic 3 & Knuckles he plays Tails' game.
- **Stinger:** press Y in mid-air, even while flying, to dart ahead. Hold up or down as you press it to aim the dart 45 degrees up or down. It hits enemies, once per jump; a dart ends his flight for that jump.

### Espio the Chameleon

- **Whirlwind:** press jump in mid-air to whirl in place, floating down briefly and hitting enemies all around.
- **Leaf Swirl:** press Y to whirl and turn invisible for a second and a half: enemies and hazards pass right through him. It needs a few seconds to recharge.

### Vector the Crocodile

- **Shoulder Dash:** press jump in mid-air to charge straight ahead. Hold up to charge straight up, or down to charge diagonally down. It hits enemies.
- **Wall breaker:** he smashes through breakable walls by walking into them, like Knuckles (Sonic 1, Sonic 2, Sonic CD and Sonic 3 & Knuckles, where that includes Knuckles' rock piles and ice columns too).
- **Physics:** heavy: a little slower to get going than Sonic.

### Cream the Rabbit

- **Flight:** Cream flies like Tails, flapping her ears: press jump in mid-air, and keep pressing it to climb, until she tires out.
- **Chao Attack:** press Y to send Cheese out. He flies ahead and curves toward the nearest enemy on screen. He hits the first badnik, boss or monitor he touches, then flies back to her. With no enemy around he goes about 100 px ahead and comes back. He flies through walls. One Cheese at a time, with a short wait after he's back.

### Dr. Robotnik

- **Rocket Ride:** press jump in mid-air and he blasts forward on his rocket pack for half a second, fast and level, smashing enemies and monitors in his way. Then the rocket explodes: the blast hits enemies all around him and pops him upward, a bit higher than his jump. Flying into a wall blows it up early; getting hurt or hitting a spring ends it with no blast. Once per jump.
- **Parachute:** keep holding jump after the blast; once he starts falling, his parachute pops and he drifts down.
- **Egg Claw:** press Y to throw his giant Sonic CD glove. It flies ahead, spinning, then comes back to him like a boomerang, through walls. It breaks enemies and monitors and hits bosses; one at a time.
- **Bomb Drop:** hold down and press Y (crouching, or holding down in the air) to drop his 16-ton weight. It bounces along the ground ahead of him and breaks enemies and monitors and hits bosses; up to two at a time. In mid-air, down + Y always drops it: plain Y still turns him Super when he can.
- He jumps, rolls and Spin Dashes in a red spin ball. **Physics:** heavy and slow to get going.

### Max the Rabbit

- **Ear Copter:** press jump in mid-air and he whirls his ears and floats down slowly while you hold jump, for about a second and a half per jump.
- **Ear Grapple:** press Y in mid-air and his ear shoots out diagonally, up and forward. If it catches a wall, a ledge or a ceiling, it reels him in to that spot and lets go with a little hop, so he can zip up to ledges. Enemies and monitors it reaches get hit; if it catches nothing, it snaps back. Every catch (or enemy hit) gives the grapple back after a quarter-second pause, so you can chain grabs up between walls; a miss uses it up until you land. It works out of the Ear Copter too, and after it you can hold jump to copter down again.
- **Physics:** a springy rabbit: a higher jump and quick on his feet.

### Tikal the Echidna

- **Spirit Flight:** press jump in mid-air and she dissolves into a glowing spirit orb, then flies wherever you point the d-pad (in all eight directions) for about a second and a half. The orb hits enemies and breaks monitors. Press jump again to re-form early; landing, getting hit or touching a spring also ends it. Once per jump.
- **Spirit Orb:** press Y and she casts a glowing spirit orb straight ahead. It flies a short way (about 200 pixels), hitting the first enemy, monitor or boss in its way; walls stop it. Two can be out at once, with a short recharge between casts. She stops to cast on the ground; in the air she keeps her speed.

### Mario

- **Triple Jump** (as in Super Mario 64): while running, jump again right as you land to jump higher, and a third time for an even higher jump with a somersault. Standing still, walking slowly, waiting too long after landing, rolling or getting hit starts the count over. He has no mid-air move of his own: pressing jump again in the air does nothing.
- **Fireball:** press Y to throw a fireball that flies a good way ahead of him, hitting enemies and monitors on its way. He stops to throw it on the ground; in the air he keeps his speed. One at a time.
- Mario jumps the classic way, fist in the air, instead of curling into a ball, but he still curls up when he rolls along the ground or lets go of a Spin Dash, and his jump still hits enemies like Sonic's. He has Sonic's physics.

### Trip the Sungazer

- **Double Jump** (as in Sonic Superstars): press jump in mid-air to jump again, spinning in her armored shell. The spin hits enemies and breaks monitors until she starts to fall. Once per jump.
- **Wall Cling:** jump into a wall while holding toward it and she clings to it for about a second and a half, sliding slowly down after the first half second. She holds on as long as you hold toward the wall: add up or down to climb, and climb past the top to hop up onto the ledge. Press jump to kick off the wall (her Double Jump is ready again, so she can go up a shaft wall to wall), or let go of the direction to drop off. Landing, getting hit or touching a spring ends it.
- She has Sonic's physics.

### E-102 Gamma

- **Hover:** press jump in mid-air to fire his jets, and hold jump to hover, sinking slowly while you steer left and right, for up to 2 seconds per jump. Let go of jump to drop; he can't hover again until he lands. The hover doesn't attack.
- **Arm Cannon:** press Y to fire an electric bolt (the Lightning Shield's spark), aimed with the d-pad: on the ground back, up, forward or the diagonals between; in the air all eight ways. With no direction held it fires the way he faces. It flies straight until it hits something, reaches a wall, floor or ceiling, or runs out; it breaks enemies and monitors and hits bosses. Up to three at a time.
- **Physics:** heavy: slower to get going, a lower top speed and a slightly lower jump than Sonic.
- **Robot:** he never needs to breathe underwater, in all four games.
- He never curls into a ball: he doesn't roll (holding down while running does nothing) and has no Spin Dash (down + jump is a plain jump). He can still crouch, and his jump still hits enemies like Sonic's.

### Jet the Hawk

- **Extreme Gear:** press jump in mid-air to snap onto his hoverboard and air-surf, Sonic Riders style, while you hold jump, for up to about 3.5 seconds per ride. He rides fast (at least as fast as a Jet Dash, and faster if he already was), sinking only slowly. Hold forward to speed up; hold back to brake, and keep holding it to carve round and ride the other way. Hold up to tilt the board's nose up for a little lift (about three quarters of a second per ride). The board rams through enemies and breaks monitors. Letting go of jump, running out of ride time, hitting a wall, getting hit or touching a spring knocks him off; landing sets him running at the board's speed. Once per jump.
- **Tornado Trap:** press Y and he whips up a whirlwind ahead of him. It drifts slowly forward for two seconds, even if he races on, and hits the first enemy, monitor or boss that touches it; walls stop it. One at a time, with a short recharge. On the ground he stops to swing; in the air he keeps his speed.
- **Physics:** a racer: a higher top speed and quicker to get going than Sonic.

### Mecha Sonic

- **Spike Ball:** press jump in mid-air and he curls into his spiky ball and slams diagonally down and forward, as in his Sonic & Knuckles boss fight, until he lands. It smashes enemies and monitors, bouncing off them like a jump. Once per jump.
- **Jet Boost:** press Y for a short afterburner burst: a quick dash forward, on the ground or level in the air, that smashes enemies in his way. It needs a moment to recharge between boosts. (In Sonic CD, looking up and pressing jump also does it on the ground.)
- **Spike Ball Throw:** hold down and press Y (crouching, or holding down in the air) to throw a spiked ball, his own spinning spike ball, that bounces along the ground ahead of him. It breaks enemies and monitors and hits bosses; up to two at a time. In mid-air, down + Y always throws: plain Y still turns him Super when he can.
- **Robot:** he never needs to breathe underwater, in all four games.
- He rolls, jumps and Spin Dashes in his spiky ball. He has Sonic's physics.

### Sticks the Badger

- **Boomerang:** press Y to throw her boomerang. It flies out ahead of her, turns and comes back to her, hitting enemies and breaking monitors on the way. Only one is out at a time.
- **Wall Cling:** jump at a wall and she grabs on. She can hold on for a moment, climb up or down, and jump off.
- She jumps and Spin Dashes in Tails' spin ball, in her own colours.

### Chaos

- **Puddle Slide:** press jump in mid-air and he drops straight down. When he lands he melts into a puddle, slides forward along the ground for a moment and rises back up. Nothing can hurt him while he's a puddle. Jumping, rolling or springs end it early. Once per jump.
- **Swim:** underwater, pressing jump in mid-air is a swim stroke instead of the Puddle Slide: each press pushes him upward, as often as you like.
- **Stretch Punch:** press Y and his arm stretches out to punch far ahead of him, on the ground or in the air. It hits enemies and breaks monitors along its whole length. On the ground he stops to punch.
- **Water body:** he never needs to breathe underwater, in all four games.
- He jumps and Spin Dashes in a spin ball in his own blues. He has Sonic's physics.

### Flicky

- **Flight:** Flicky flies like Tails, flapping his wings: press jump in mid-air, and keep pressing it to climb, until he tires out. Underwater he swims. In Sonic 3 & Knuckles he plays Tails' game.
- **Animal Drop:** press Y to drop one of his little animal friends (a chicken, a squirrel, a penguin, a pig, a rabbit or a seal: a different one each time) from under him. It falls with his speed and flattens any enemy it lands on, and breaks monitors too. It vanishes when it lands. Up to three at a time. It works while flying, jumping or standing (standing, he tosses it a little way ahead). (In Sonic 1 and Sonic 2 only the first four animals take turns.)
- He jumps and Spin Dashes in his own spin ball. He has Tails' physics.

### Tails Doll

- **Phase Warp:** press jump in mid-air and he flickers out of sight and reappears up to four tiles away, in the direction you hold on the d-pad (any of eight ways; nothing held: straight ahead). Nothing can hurt him while he warps. He never warps into or through walls, floors or ceilings: he stops short at the last clear spot. Once per jump.
- **Screen Nuke:** press Y and he spreads his arms, then his head opens on a glowing red core: the screen flashes black and every enemy and monitor on the screen is hit at once (bosses take one hit). He stands still for it (in the air he hangs in place), and nothing can hurt him meanwhile. It takes 10 seconds to recharge. (In Sonic CD, looking up and pressing jump also does it on the ground.)
- He jumps and Spin Dashes in his own ball. He has Sonic's physics (and his Super Peel-Out in Sonic CD).

### Bean the Dynamite

- **Leap:** press jump in mid-air to leap again, a second jump that hits enemies until he starts to fall. Once per jump.
- **Bomb Throw:** press Y to throw a bomb that bounces along the ground ahead of him, hitting enemies and monitors. Hold up as you press Y to lob it high instead. Up to two at a time. He stops to throw on the ground; in the air he keeps his speed.
- He jumps and Spin Dashes in his own spin ball. He has Sonic's physics.

### Bark the Polar Bear

- **Slam Ground** (Mighty's Hammer Drop): press jump in mid-air to slam straight down, fist raised. Landing bounces him back up in his ball and sends two shockwaves of dust racing along the ground, one each way, following slopes and hitting the enemies and monitors in their path. They stop at walls, at the edge of a drop or after a short distance. It hits enemies on the way down too.
- **Bear Rush:** press Y for a short, fast shoulder charge the way he faces, on the ground or level through the air, smashing enemies and monitors in his way. It recharges in a second. (In Sonic CD, looking up and pressing jump also does it on the ground.)
- **Physics:** heavy: slower to get going, a lower top speed and a slightly lower jump than Sonic.
- He jumps and Spin Dashes in his own spin ball.

### Heavy

- **Wall breaker:** like Knuckles, he smashes breakable walls just by walking into them, in all four games. In Sonic 3 & Knuckles that includes Knuckles' other barriers: Angel Island's and Lava Reef's rock piles and Ice Cap's ice columns.
- **Juggernaut Charge:** hold Y on the ground and he shoves off at once, locks on and charges the way he faces, faster and faster, far past his normal top speed (a flash bursts in front of his fist once he's past it, about a quarter-second in). Let go of Y and he keeps sliding, slowing down only very gradually (holding back brakes a little harder) until he's back to his normal speed. The whole time he smashes enemies, monitors and bosses in his way, and still breaks walls. A solid wall stops him; jumping keeps his speed.
- **Unstoppable:** while the charge is past his normal top speed (charging or sliding), enemies can't hurt him: spiky badniks, badniks' shots and bosses' parts are harmless (some spiky badniks just break), and bosses take the hit. Hazards still hurt: spikes, crushers, lava, lasers and pits.
- **Shine Spark** (Super Metroid style): while the charge is past his top speed on the ground, press **down** to store it. He skids to a stop and glows white-hot for about 4 seconds. Meanwhile he can walk and jump as usual, but he can't charge or roll. Press **jump** while he glows, on the ground or in the air, to launch: straight **up** (nothing held, or up), **diagonally** (up + forward) or **forward** (forward alone). He flies at a constant 12 pixels per frame with no gravity and no control until a wall, ceiling or floor stops him; then he drops. While flying he smashes enemies, monitors and bosses, enemies can't hurt him (hazards still can), and he breaks walls. If the glow runs out, or he's hit, the stored charge is lost.
- **Physics:** heavy: slower to get going, a lower top speed and a slightly lower jump than Sonic. No mid-air move (the Shine Spark aside).
- He jumps and Spin Dashes in a spin ball in his own greys (his sheet has none).

### Bomb

- **Hop:** press jump in mid-air to hop again, a second jump that hits enemies until he starts to fall. Once per jump.
- **Self-Destruct:** press Y and he detonates in a huge explosion that destroys enemies and monitors and hits bosses across about half the screen around him, then pulls himself back together. Nothing can hurt him during the blast, but it costs him: afterwards he takes a normal hit, dropping his rings (or losing his shield instead). With no rings, it's his last act: he blows himself up for good. He stands still for it on the ground; in the air he keeps his speed. It takes one second to recharge.
- He jumps and Spin Dashes in a spin ball in his own black. He's tiny, and has Sonic's physics.

### Honey the Cat

- **Spin Attack:** press Y on the ground or in the air and she whirls round in a dark spin for as long as you hold Y (up to about a second and a half, then a short breather before the next). Spinning, she smashes enemies, monitors and bosses, and bounces hard off whatever she hits, like a pinball (on the ground she's knocked up and back). In the air, and if she jumps while spinning, she's floaty: she falls at half her usual weight for as long as the spin lasts. On the ground she keeps moving as she was.
- She jumps and Spin Dashes in her own ball. She has Sonic's physics.

### E-123 Omega

- **Flame Shot:** tap Y and he fires a small flame from his arm cannon, straight ahead, until it hits an enemy, a monitor, a boss or a wall. One at a time, about one a second.
- **Flame Blast:** hold Y to charge (he can walk meanwhile). After half a second he starts glowing red; after a second and a half the glow flashes orange and white: the charge is full. Let go and he fires his big fireball, which burns straight through enemies, monitors and bosses (hitting each once) until it reaches a wall. Letting go early fires nothing more (the tap already fired its small flame), and getting hit loses the charge. After a blast the cannon needs three seconds to cool down before it fires again. He plants his feet to fire on the ground; in the air he keeps his speed.
- **Jet Hover:** press jump in mid-air to fire his jets and hover, sinking slowly while you hold jump (up to two seconds per jump).
- **Wall breaker:** like Knuckles, he smashes breakable walls just by walking into them, in all four games (in Sonic 3 & Knuckles, Knuckles' rock piles and ice columns too).
- **Robot:** he never drowns.
- **Physics:** heavy: slower to get going, a lower top speed and a slightly lower jump than Sonic.
- Like Gamma, he never curls into a ball: he jumps in his own mid-air pose (it still hits enemies), and has no Spin Dash.

### Sally Acorn

- **Spin-Kick High Jump:** press Y on the ground or in the air. After a short wind-up she somersaults straight up in a blue swirl of kicks, much higher than her jump (great for bosses up high), hitting whatever she touches on the way up. She can be hit during the wind-up and while she comes upright at the top; then she falls as from a jump. Once per jump in the air; on the ground there's a short wait before the next one.
- **Flying Kick Dive:** press jump in mid-air and she dives down and forward in a flying kick, hitting enemies on the way (like Mecha Sonic's Spike Ball). It isn't a double jump.
- She jumps and Spin Dashes in her own ball. She has Sonic's physics.

### Marine the Raccoon

- **Anchor Throw:** press Y and she throws her anchor forward on a chain, in an arc: up, then falling (hold up as you press Y to throw it higher). It hits enemies, monitors and bosses along the way. If it bites into a wall or a ceiling, the chain reels her in to it, and she lets go with a little hop at the end: great for crossing gaps and reaching ledges. If it lands on a floor or reaches the end of its chain, she pulls it back and it's gone. One anchor at a time, with a short wait after. She stands still to throw on the ground; in the air she keeps falling.
- **Sea legs:** above water, the surface is solid ground for her: she can stand, walk, run, roll and jump on it. Hold down to dive in. Underwater she swims as usual, and she never drowns. Coming up from below, she jumps out and lands on the water as she falls back. Rising water carries her up (unless there's a ceiling in the way; then she stays under).
- No mid-air move. She jumps and Spin Dashes in her own ball, and has Sonic's physics.

### Mephiles the Dark

- **Floating:** he never walks: he floats everywhere, standing, walking and running, and leans forward the faster he goes (upright when still, most at top speed). He doesn't tilt with slopes.
- **Crystal Shot:** press Y and he throws two big dark crystal orbs at once, one ahead and one behind. Each dips down first, levels out, then arcs up once and vanishes (about 190 pixels away), hitting the first enemy, monitor or boss in its way. They fly straight through walls, floors and ceilings. One pair at a time, and it takes two seconds to recharge. He stops to throw on the ground; in the air he keeps his speed.
- **Shadow Sink:** on the ground, hold down and press Y: he sinks into a dark pool and waits under the ground as purple smoke. Nothing can hurt him while he's sinking, under or rising, but he can't move either. Keep holding down and Y to stay under, for up to two seconds; let go of either to rise early. After he rises, it takes a second before he can sink again.
- **Float:** press jump in mid-air to float for a moment (up to half a second while you hold jump), sinking slowly, flying flat as he does at full speed.
- He jumps and Spin Dashes in Sonic Mania's spin ball, in his colours. He has Sonic's physics.

### Emerl

## Known issues and limits

- **Sonic 3 & Knuckles:** extras play Sonic's version of the game (his story and his stages). Changing a slot's character on the save screen takes effect when that slot is played.
- **Ice Cap snowboard (Sonic 3 & Knuckles):** extras turn into Sonic for the snowboard ride at the start of the zone.
- **Special stages:** the sprite sheets have no views from behind, so in the Sonic 2, Sonic CD and Sonic 3 & Knuckles (Blue Spheres) special stages the extras roll through as their spin ball (Gamma and Omega, who have no ball, go through in their jump pose).
- **Placeholder art:** special stage results leave the extras' names out ("GOT THEM ALL"), and the Sonic 1 special stage results show Sonic's icon. In Sonic 2's ending, the extras appear as Sonic.
- **Missions:** extras can't be used in Missions.
- **Other mods:** mods that change the same game scripts or Origins' menu files (`ui_mainmenu.pac`, `ui_gamestage.pac`, `text_menu_*.pac`), such as character mods, menu mods and gameplay overhauls, may not work together with NoSwap. At startup NoSwap writes copies of its own menu files with every installed character's card (picture and name) into its `cache` folder (about 120 MB, rewritten only when the characters or NoSwap change), and the game is given those.
- **Cards:** Origins' character select has room for 64 extras' cards; any beyond that show their base character's picture and no name.
- **Scrolling:** in Origins' character select, holding left/right stops at the edge (press again to scroll), and the mouse only reaches the visible cards.
